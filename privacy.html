Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

The Falkners Arms Golf Society is run by its committee, and the committee looks after the personal information on this website and in the society app. This page explains what we hold, why, who can see it and what you can ask us to do with it. If anything here isn't clear, get in touch.

What we hold

  • Your account: your name, the email address you sign in with, and your membership status
  • Your golf: handicap, scores, results, prizes and which rounds you've entered, including whether you've paid
  • Things you post: your profile picture, photos, posts, comments and messages in the Clubhouse and on the round feed
  • Details you choose to add: your mobile number, email address, date of birth and home address. These are optional, and you can change or remove them at any time on your profile in the app
  • Notifications: which notifications you've switched on, and the code your phone gives us so we can send them
  • Messages to us: anything you send through the contact or joining forms

Why we hold it

To run the society: organising fixtures and tee times, scoring rounds, keeping the leaderboard and handicaps, collecting payments and keeping members in touch. Your optional details are only held because you added them, and only shown to the people you choose.

Who can see what

  • Members: can see names, handicaps, scores, results, prizes, profile pictures and Clubhouse posts
  • Your optional details: you choose for each one whether it's seen by only you or by members. Members only ever see the day and month of your birthday, never the year
  • Direct messages and group chats: only the people in that chat can read them. If a message is reported, the committee sees that message so they can deal with it
  • The committee: can always see your optional details, so they can contact you about the society. They don't share them outside the society
  • The public: fixtures, results and the leaderboard on this website can be seen by anyone, with members' names

Where it's kept

Your information is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in London. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and the contact and joining forms are handled by Formspree. When you use the app, it also loads maps (Esri and OpenStreetMap), weather for the course (Open-Meteo) and fonts (Google Fonts). Those services see your device's internet address, as any website does, but we don't send them your personal details. Notifications are delivered through Apple or Google, depending on your phone.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and details while you're a member. Results, scores and prizes stay as part of the society's history. If you leave, we'll remove your optional details, photos and account when you ask, and can take your name off past results if you'd prefer.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also object to how we use it. Just contact the committee. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
