Who we are

Great mates, warm welcomes, no airs and graces.

We welcome players of all standards and guarantee a warm reception and a great day out. The Society was founded with the aim of playing ten events throughout the year, including a Ryder Cup-style tournament. We play some excellent courses across southern England while keeping costs reasonable - and usually fail miserably to get that dimpled little ball anywhere near the right hole. Couldn't be better.

Order of Merit

Nine rounds count toward the season leaderboard. Every event, every point, tracked automatically.

Hole in One Club

Starting 2027, £1 a head goes into the pot at every meeting. Whoever lands that first hole-in-one clears their bar tab.

2027 Fines System

Every misdemeanour - on or off the course - goes straight to the Captain. Expect laughs, light chaos, and fines that fuel the season’s trophies, awards, and future events. Game on.
