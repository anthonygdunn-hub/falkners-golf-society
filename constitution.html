How the society is run

Constitution

Draft for adoption at the AGM in January 2027. Members can suggest changes to the committee before then. Once it is adopted, it can only be changed as set out in section 12.

1. Name

The society is called The Falkners Arms Golf Society ("the Society"). It was founded in 2023 and is based at The Falkners Arms, Fleet.

2. Aims

  1. To organise friendly, competitive golf for members of all standards, at courses around Hampshire, Surrey and Berkshire
  2. To run a season of meetings, with an Order of Merit, prizes and side competitions
  3. To keep members in touch and enjoy the social side of the game, before and after the round

3. Membership

  1. Anyone can apply to join through the society website
  2. The committee decides on each application. Membership starts once the committee has approved it
  3. Members are expected to hold an official handicap, or to be working towards one. The committee will set a starting handicap for anyone who doesn't have one yet
  4. A member can leave at any time by telling the Secretary
  5. The committee can suspend or end a membership for serious or repeated breaches of this constitution or the society rules, or for conduct that brings the Society into disrepute. The member will be told why and given the chance to put their side to the committee before any decision is made

4. Fees and money

  1. There is no annual membership fee
  2. For each meeting, players pay the fee set for that fixture, plus the prize pot and any other contributions the committee has agreed (such as the Hole in One Club's £1 a head)
  3. Fees must be paid by the date the committee sets for each round. The committee can give an unpaid place to someone on the waiting list
  4. The Society's money is held in a bank account in the Society's name. The Treasurer looks after it and keeps a record of everything paid in and out
  5. Round fees, prizes and the Hole in One pot are paid out as normal business. Any other spending needs the agreement of at least two committee officers
  6. The Society is not run for profit. Any money left over is used for the benefit of members, for example towards prizes or the end-of-season event
  7. The Treasurer presents the accounts for the year at each AGM

5. The committee

  1. The Society is run by a committee of four officers: Chairman, Vice-Chairman, Secretary and Treasurer
  2. Officers are elected each year at the AGM and serve until the next AGM. They can stand again
  3. Any member can be nominated for a role, as long as the nomination is seconded by another member and the person nominated agrees to stand
  4. If a role becomes vacant between AGMs, the committee can appoint a member to fill it until the next AGM
  5. The committee meets whenever it needs to, in person or online. Three officers make a quorum. Decisions are made by majority, and the Chairman has a casting vote if it's tied
  6. The officers' main duties are:
    • Chairman: leads the committee, chairs the AGM and committee meetings, and represents the Society
    • Vice-Chairman: supports the Chairman and stands in when the Chairman isn't available
    • Secretary: handles membership, fixtures, communications with members and venues, notice of meetings and the minutes
    • Treasurer: looks after the Society's money, collects fees, pays venues and prizes, and keeps the accounts

6. Annual General Meeting

  1. The AGM is held every January, before the new season starts
  2. The Secretary gives all members at least 21 days' notice of the date, time and place, along with any proposals to be voted on
  3. Members who want something discussed or voted on should send it to the Secretary at least 14 days before the AGM
  4. The AGM will:
    • Hear the Chairman's report on the past season
    • Receive the Treasurer's accounts
    • Elect the committee for the coming year
    • Agree the outline of the coming season
    • Vote on any proposals, including changes to this constitution
  5. The AGM can go ahead if at least 10 members, or a third of all members (whichever is fewer), are there in person or online
  6. Every member present has one vote. Decisions are made by a simple majority of those voting, unless this constitution says otherwise. The Chairman has a casting vote if it's tied

7. Extraordinary General Meetings

  1. The committee can call an Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM) at any time
  2. The committee must call one within 28 days if at least five members ask for one in writing, saying what they want discussed
  3. Members get at least 14 days' notice of an EGM. It follows the same quorum and voting rules as the AGM

8. Competitions and handicaps

  1. Rounds are played under the Rules of Golf and the rules of the host course
  2. Each member has a society handicap. It starts from their England Golf handicap index and is recalculated after each round, under the method set out in the society rules
  3. Players can't change their own handicap. Only the committee can change a handicap, and the committee's decision is final
  4. Unless the committee says otherwise for a particular round, the Society plays off the yellow tees with a 75% handicap allowance
  5. The formats for each round, and how many rounds count towards the Order of Merit, are set by the committee and published before the season starts
  6. Each player is responsible for their scorecard. If a score is disputed and the group can't agree, the committee decides, and its decision is final
  7. The Hole in One Club pot is only paid out to a member, and only for a hole in one at an Order of Merit event

9. Rounds, guests and the waiting list

  1. Each round is capped at 32 players, including guests
  2. Members are welcome to bring guests. A member is responsible for their guests' fees and conduct on the day
  3. Once a round is full, further entries go on a waiting list. The committee decides who moves up when a place becomes free
  4. Guests can't win the Hole in One Club pot

10. Conduct

Members and guests are expected to play in the right spirit, keep up the pace of play, respect the courses we visit and follow each host club's dress code and etiquette. We play as guests of other clubs, and how we behave decides whether we're invited back.

11. Society rules

The committee can make, change and publish society rules covering the day-to-day running of the Society, such as entry and payment deadlines, handicap calculation, prizes and competition formats. Society rules can't contradict this constitution. They are published on the society website, and members are told when they change.

12. Changing this constitution

  1. This constitution can only be changed at an AGM or EGM
  2. Any proposed change must be sent to members with the notice of the meeting
  3. A change needs the support of at least two-thirds of the members voting

13. Personal information

The committee looks after members' personal information as set out in the privacy notice, and only uses it to run the Society.

14. Winding up the society

  1. The Society can be wound up by a vote of at least two-thirds of the members voting at an AGM or EGM called for that purpose
  2. Any outstanding debts are paid first. Any money left over, including the Hole in One pot, is given to a charity or local cause chosen at that meeting

Questions about the constitution? Contact the committee.
